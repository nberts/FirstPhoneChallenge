Back in 2003, my parents finally gave in and got me my very first cell phone - the legendary LG PM325! This little beauty wasn't just any phone, it was my gateway to teenage coolness. The best part? I figured out how to program the Beverly Hills 90210 theme song as my ringtone, and let me tell you, heads would turn in the mall food court whenever someone called! 😎


Sure, it didn't have a fancy camera or internet access, but it had SNAKE, and those blue LED lights on the side would blink with every notification. Plus, the flip action made me feel like I was in The Matrix every time I answered a call. The T9 texting made me the fastest thumb-typer in my friend group, and I spent hours personalizing the perfect combination of ringtones and wallpapers.


Storage space? A whopping 20 text messages! And don't even get me started on the battery life - this bad boy could last almost a week on a single charge. Those were the days when phones were phones, and accidentally dropping it was more likely to damage the floor than the phone!
